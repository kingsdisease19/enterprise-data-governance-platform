# System Modules

1. **Dashboard** — high-level overview: dataset counts, quality scores, recent activity
2. **Data Catalog** — browse/search all registered datasets
3. **Metadata Management** — schema, columns, source system, update frequency
4. **Business Glossary** — shared definitions of business terms (e.g., "Active Customer")
5. **Data Dictionary** — column-level definitions across datasets
6. **Data Quality** — rules, checks, and results/scorecards
7. **Data Lineage** — where data comes from and where it flows to
8. **Governance Policies** — access rules, retention rules, classification levels
9. **Reports** — compliance reports, quality trends, ownership coverage
10. **Administration** — manage users, roles, and permissions
